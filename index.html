video-pipeline

A personal tool for making and uploading short videos.

video-pipeline runs on its author's own computer. It assembles short videos from public-domain and openly licensed material — museum open-access collections, Wikimedia Commons, NASA imagery, and public-domain text — and uploads them to that person's own YouTube channels.

Who can use it

Nobody else. It is a single-user tool with no website, no accounts, no sign-up and no server. It is not distributed, and it is not usable by anyone other than its author.

Google account access

It asks for permission to upload videos to its owner's own YouTube channel, and for read-only access so it can confirm those uploads arrived. It collects no data from anyone. The privacy policy sets out exactly what it touches and what it stores, and the terms cover use of the tool itself.

Contact

Questions go to the support address shown on the application's Google consent screen.
