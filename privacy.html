Privacy Policy

Last updated: 30 September 2026

This policy covers video-pipeline, a personal tool used by its owner to upload videos to their own YouTube channel.

What this application is

video-pipeline is a private, single-user tool. It runs on its owner's own computer and is not offered to, installed by, or usable by anyone else.

What data it accesses

The application requests two Google API permissions:

  • https://www.googleapis.com/auth/youtube.upload — permission to upload videos to the account owner's own YouTube channel.
  • https://www.googleapis.com/auth/youtube.readonly — read-only access to that same channel. This is requested only so the tool can confirm which channel a token is bound to and that its own uploads arrived there. youtube.upload alone cannot read, so it cannot verify its own work.

Neither scope permits deleting videos, changing channel settings, posting comments, or acting on any account other than the owner's own.

What data it collects

None.

The application has no users other than its owner, no accounts, no sign-up, no analytics, no telemetry, and no server. It does not collect, receive, store, transmit, sell, or share personal information from anyone.

What it stores, and where

An OAuth token issued by Google is stored in a single file on the owner's own computer, so the tool does not have to request permission on every run. That file never leaves that computer. No other credential or personal data is stored.

Third parties

The application communicates with:

  • Google / YouTube — to upload videos to the owner's own channel, and to confirm they arrived. Governed by Google's own privacy policy.
  • Wikimedia Commons and Wikipedia — public-domain and openly licensed images, and article text.
  • NASA — public-domain imagery.
  • The Metropolitan Museum of Art, the Art Institute of Chicago and the Cleveland Museum of Art — open-access images of public-domain works in their collections.
  • Internet Archive — public-domain media.
  • Christian Classics Ethereal Library and the helloao.org Bible API — public-domain scripture and commentary text.

Every one of these is an unauthenticated read request for material the source publishes openly. No personal information is sent to any of them, and none of them is sent anything about anyone.

YouTube API Services

This application uses YouTube API Services to upload videos to its owner's own channel.

By authorising it, its owner agrees to the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's own handling of any data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

### Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time, in either of two ways:

  • at Google's security settings page, which ends the application's access to the account immediately; or
  • by deleting the stored token file from the computer the application runs on, which leaves it with no credential at all.

No request to anyone is needed for either. There is no server holding a copy, and no account to close.

### Data retention

The only stored item is the OAuth token described above, kept on the owner's own computer until it is deleted or expires. Nothing is retained anywhere else, because nothing is sent anywhere else.

Children

The application is not directed at children and collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes

Any change to this policy will be published on this page with a revised date above.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to the support address listed on the application's Google OAuth consent screen.
